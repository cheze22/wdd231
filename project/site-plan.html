Eco-Adventure Hub Site Plan

Author: Cristhian Nefi Dorado Aguilar

Site Name

Eco-Adventure Hub

Reasoning: This name was chosen to position the website as a central, reliable "hub" for eco-conscious travelers and outdoor enthusiasts, emphasizing sustainability and community exploration.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Eco-Adventure Hub is to provide a specialized platform for sustainable tourism. It offers curated ecological trail maps, expert advice on biodegradable gear, and a registration system for community-led environmental conservation expeditions.

Target Audience Scenarios

Scenario 1: "I am looking for a hiking trail that is beginner-friendly but also has a high 'Eco-Rating' to ensure my visit has minimal impact on local wildlife. Where can I find this data?"

Scenario 2: "I want to join a group of like-minded explorers for a weekend cleanup hike. Is there a calendar or registration form for upcoming events?"

Color Scheme

The palette is designed to reflect the organic tones of the forest and earth:

Primary: #2D5A27 (Forest Green)
Accent: #E07A5F (Terra Cotta)
Background: #F4F1DE (Cream)

Typography

Montserrat - Headings

Open Sans - Body copy for high readability and professional aesthetic.
